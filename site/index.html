My ad script briefs

Every brief starts with one table: the concept, who it's for, the format, where the ad sends people, the hypothesis, and what we do if it wins or loses.

Then come the notes for the video editor and the full script with its hooks.

The first things I do when I come on board

  1. I watch your winning ads

    I break them down by format, hook, awareness stage and more. Then I look for patterns.

  2. I fill the gaps

    If I see a gap, I create an ad that fills it, with a hypothesis.

  3. I brief your editors

    I write the scripts and briefs your video editors need.

  4. I test and build on what wins

    I check NC CPA, CTR and hook rate, then build the next round on the winners.
