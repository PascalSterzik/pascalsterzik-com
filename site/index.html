Tools I use

Ad scripts delivered on a silver platter

I add an extra pound of attention to detail so that even a blindfolded toddler could turn these into winning video ads.

  • Every part gets its own row. The visual, what happens, and the line. The video editor doesn't have to guess how it should look like. He simply has to turn it to life.

  • Every character gets a character sheet showing them from multiple angles so they look the same in every shot. Many miss this and wonder why their character switches their haircut and outfit from scene to scene. No bueno.

  • Every place gets a set sheet and a seating chart, so rooms, doors and seats stay the same from shot to shot. Again, you don't want random chairs appearing like it's WWE.

  • Every image in the script sits in a folder, numbered and named by hook and row so your video editor can find, download, and hand each image to Higgsfield. or Claude. Or ChatGPT. I don't care. I just want a great video ad.

  • All the info you need, and nothing you don't. Read this to get the big idea of the ad.

  • This gives clear, more detailed instructions for the video editor so he can turn the ad script brief into a video ad more efficiently.

  • Most creative strategists just hand the creative without the primary text, and the media buyer just slaps the same three lines on it he used for all the other 387 ads. Guess what? Andromeda uses the primary text to target better. Plus, long-form copy reduces NC CPA because only interested people click. Same with the headline and button. It's all underrated.

  • I don't make things up. You can find the source for every claim in here. We're just little snowflakes... we'd never lie to our customers, would we? 😙🎶
