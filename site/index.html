When I join your team

  1. I study your winning ads

    I watch your best ads and break them down by format, hook, angle and awareness stage. Then I look for patterns.

  2. I find the gaps

    When I spot a gap, like a buyer or awareness stage your ads skip, I make an ad that fills it, with a clear hypothesis.

  3. I learn how your buyers talk

    I read your reviews, comments and your competitors' ads, so the ads use your buyers' own words.

  4. I brief your team

    I write the statics, scripts and briefs, so your editors and creators know what to make.

  5. I test and build on what wins

    I check NC CPA, CTR and hook rate, then build the next round on the winners.
